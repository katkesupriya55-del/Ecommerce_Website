Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to ShopEasy. We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit or make a purchase from our website.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit or interact with ShopEasy, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and billing details provided during checkout or account creation.
  • Order Information: Products viewed, cart items, purchase history, and payment method details (processed securely via encrypted gateways).
  • Device Data: IP address, browser type, device type, and browsing interaction logs through standard cookies.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively to provide and improve our services, including:

  • Processing, fulfilling, and shipping your orders.
  • Sending order confirmations, tracking details, and customer support updates.
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions and ensuring website security.
  • Sending promotional offers and updates (only if you opt-in).

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

ShopEasy uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to store your preferences, maintain your shopping cart, and personalize your experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not function properly.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share essential information with trusted third-party service providers required to operate our business, such as payment processors, shipping carriers, and IT infrastructure services.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can update your account settings directly or reach out to us at any time to request data deletion or opt out of marketing communications.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our privacy team at privacy@shopeasy.com or through our Contact Us page.
