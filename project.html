    About US

    In 1987, a small shop was born in Amreli out of the desire to build sweet memories of joy and happiness to the citizens of the town. Slowly, as the town evolved, so did Sheetal Ice Creams. Now, over 30 years later, we look back at our journey from that one shop to a multi distribution network, our hearts ﬁll with gratitude towards the love that people have shown us. We use state of the art technology and modern machinery to transport, prepare, package and distribute your favourite ice creams. Your support and faith in us propels us to reach further heights each day. Sheetal Cool Products Limited is an SME, BSE listed and ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 22000:2018 certified company. It is now among less than 10 companies in India with 6 or more food categories approved with BRCG & U. S. FDA modules under a single plant. It also holds 90th rank, as per Economic Times, among the top 150 fastest companies in India. The company is listed in Western Railway and and has also made tie up with a few fell industries.
